Prism brand guidelines

Prism is a social-commerce creator community for TikTok Shop affiliates. Tagline: See your true TikTok Shop results. This page covers the logo, colour, type, shape and how they apply to the platform, Discord and merch.

Logo system

Two marks. The icon is the i on its own: a pill striped cyan, blue, violet, magenta (top to bottom) under a small outlined prism. The wordmark is "prism" in Caprasimo with that i in place of the letter. Always lowercase. Never rebuild the i from a font glyph.

GroundsInk (#14141C) or white triangle/letters only. White or Ink backgrounds only. The stripe colours never change.

Colour

White and Ink carry most surfaces. The four spectrum colours are accents: the logo stripe, charts, tags and highlights. Use them in this order whenever they appear together.

Usage rules

DoKeep the stripe order cyan → blue → violet → magenta, top to bottom.
Don’tRecolour, rotate, stretch or add effects (glow, gradient, shadow) to the i.
DoUse the SVG files or the PrismLogo component. Both draw the i from geometry.
Don’tWrite the wordmark in capitals or swap Caprasimo for another face.
DoUse spectrum colours for charts and highlights, in order, max four per view.
Don’tUse spectrum colours for paragraph text or full-bleed backgrounds.
